LocalFold AlphaFold 3

Before the model downloads

This page runs AlphaFold 3 entirely in your browser. Pressing Fold downloads about 265 MiB of model parameters to this device and runs them on your GPU. Nothing about your sequence leaves the machine.

  • The parameters are made available for non-commercial use only, under the terms of the checkpoint they were converted from.
  • Predictions are not experimental structures. Do not rely on them for clinical, diagnostic, or safety-critical decisions.
  • A fold takes minutes and will keep your GPU busy for the whole time.
